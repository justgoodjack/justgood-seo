=== travel | How to Pack for Two Weeks in One Carry-On | carry-on-packing-two-weeks
DEK: A simple system for fitting fourteen days into a bag you never have to check, without wearing the same outfit in every photo.
QUERY: suitcase packing clothes
Checked bags cost money, slow you down at both ends of the flight, and occasionally end up in a different country. A carry-on keeps your trip moving. Two weeks fits in one if you pack for one week and plan to wash once.

## Start with a color story

Pick two neutrals, such as navy and grey, and one accent color. Every top should work with every bottom. With four tops and three bottoms that all match, you get twelve outfits from seven pieces, and nobody notices the repeats.

## The list that works

- 4 tops, one of them smart enough for a nice dinner
- 3 bottoms: one pair of jeans or trousers, one lighter pair, one pair of shorts or a skirt
- 1 warm layer, such as a merino sweater or a light fleece
- 1 packable rain jacket
- 7 pairs of underwear and 7 pairs of socks
- 2 pairs of shoes: wear the bulkier pair on the plane
- Sleepwear and swimwear if you need them

Merino wool is worth the price for travel. It dries overnight and can be worn several times before it needs a wash.

## Roll, cube, and fill the gaps

Roll soft clothes and lay structured items like shirts flat on top. Packing cubes keep the bag organized, and compression cubes can save a surprising amount of room. Stuff socks and chargers inside your shoes.

## Liquids and toiletries

Most airports limit carry-on liquids to containers of 100 ml (3.4 oz) or less, all in one clear bag. Solid versions solve most of this: shampoo bars, solid sunscreen sticks, and toothpaste tablets do not count as liquids. Buy anything bulky, like sunscreen for a beach week, when you land.

## Plan one laundry day

Around day six or seven, do a load at your accommodation or a local laundromat. Many apartments and hostels have machines, and a small bottle of travel wash handles sink washing in between.

## Weigh it at home

Airlines set their own carry-on size and weight limits, and budget carriers are the strictest. Check your airline's rules before you go, and weigh the packed bag on a bathroom scale. Leaving one item at home is easier than paying a gate fee.

=== travel | Jet Lag, Handled: A Simple Plan for Long-Haul Flights | jet-lag-plan-long-haul
DEK: Your body clock can shift about an hour a day on its own. Light, meal timing and a little planning make the first days of a trip far better.
QUERY: airplane window sunrise
Jet lag happens because your internal clock is still running on home time while the sun around you has moved on. The clock catches up by itself, roughly an hour or so per day, but you can help it along.

## Three days before you fly

Shift your bedtime toward your destination by 30 to 60 minutes a night. Flying east, go to bed earlier and get up earlier. Flying west, stay up a bit later. Even a small head start reduces how rough the first morning feels.

## On the plane

Set your watch to destination time as soon as you board, and eat and sleep according to it. If it is night where you are going, use an eye mask and earplugs and skip the movie. If it is daytime there, stay awake, keep the window shade up when you can, and walk the aisle now and then.

Drink water steadily. Cabin air is dry, and dehydration makes fatigue worse. Go easy on alcohol, which fragments sleep.

## Light is the strongest signal

Daylight is the main cue your body clock uses. After flying east, get outside in morning light and avoid bright light in the late evening. After flying west, seek out afternoon and evening light. A walk outside on your first day does more than any supplement.

## The first day on the ground

Stay up until a normal local bedtime, even if that means a long afternoon. If you must nap, keep it under 30 minutes and before mid-afternoon. Eat meals at local times so your stomach gets the same message as your eyes.

## About melatonin

Some travelers take a low dose of melatonin at local bedtime for the first few nights after flying east. It is sold over the counter in some countries and by prescription in others. Check with a doctor or pharmacist first, especially if you take other medication.

## Give yourself a soft landing

If you can, plan an easy first day: a neighborhood walk, a good lunch, nothing that needs sharp focus. Book the big tour or the important meeting for day two or three.

=== travel | The Case for Traveling in Shoulder Season | shoulder-season-travel
DEK: The weeks just before and after peak season often bring lower prices, shorter lines and weather that is still very good. Here is how to time it.
QUERY: quiet cobblestone street europe morning
Shoulder season is the stretch between a destination's busiest months and its quietest ones. For much of the Mediterranean that means April to early June and September to October. You get most of the good weather with a fraction of the crowds.

## Why it is worth it

Flights and hotels usually cost less once school holidays end. Popular sights have shorter lines, restaurants have open tables, and locals have more time to talk. In hot destinations, the milder temperatures make long walking days far more pleasant than a July heatwave.

## Find the shoulder for your destination

Every place has its own calendar. In the Caribbean, peak season is the northern winter, so late spring and early summer are quieter. In Japan, cherry blossom weeks and autumn leaves draw crowds, while early summer and late autumn are calmer. Search for "best time to visit" plus your destination and look at the months just outside the top recommendation.

## What to watch out for

- **Shorter hours.** Some beach towns and island ferries scale back after the season. Check timetables before you book.
- **Changeable weather.** Pack a light layer and a rain jacket, and keep a backup plan for a wet day.
- **Local holidays.** A quiet month can still have a busy festival weekend. Look up public holidays for your dates.

## Book smart

Set price alerts on flights a couple of months out, and compare the same trip a few weeks either side of your ideal dates. Hotels with free cancellation let you lock in a good rate and keep watching.

## A sample switch

Instead of the second week of August on the Amalfi Coast, try the last week of September. The sea is still warm from summer, the roads are calmer, and you will find a seat on the ferry without fighting for it.

=== health | The 10-Minute Walk After Meals | walk-after-meals
DEK: A short, easy walk after eating is one of the simplest habits for steadier energy and better blood sugar. You do not need gear, a gym or a plan.
QUERY: people walking park evening
Some of the best health habits are small enough to fit between other things. A gentle walk after a meal is one of them.

## What happens when you walk after eating

After a meal, blood sugar rises as your body absorbs the carbohydrates. Working muscles pull glucose out of the bloodstream for fuel, so light movement soon after eating can soften that rise. Studies of short post-meal walks have found smaller blood sugar spikes compared with sitting still, even with walks as brief as a few minutes.

Many people also find that a walk after dinner eases the heavy, sleepy feeling that follows a big meal.

## How to do it

- **Timing:** start within about 30 minutes of finishing your meal.
- **Length:** 10 to 15 minutes is plenty. Even five minutes helps.
- **Pace:** easy. You should be able to hold a conversation.

If you cannot get outside, walk around the house, climb the stairs a few times, or tidy the kitchen standing up. The point is to move instead of settling onto the couch.

## Make it stick

Attach the walk to something you already do. Finish dinner, put on your shoes, and loop the block before dishes. Invite whoever you live with, and it turns into the best conversation of the day. Some families keep a pair of walking shoes by the door just for this.

On busy workdays, a short walk after lunch also breaks up a long stretch of sitting and helps with the mid-afternoon slump.

## A note on safety

If you have diabetes, heart disease or another condition, or you take medication that affects blood sugar, ask your doctor how to fit walking into your routine.

=== health | Sleep Better Tonight: Six Habits That Help | sleep-better-habits
DEK: You cannot force sleep, but you can set up the conditions for it. These six habits are backed by sleep research and cost nothing.
QUERY: bedroom morning light bed
Most adults need seven or more hours of sleep a night, and many of us fall short. These six habits make good sleep more likely.

## 1. Wake up at the same time every day

A consistent wake time anchors your body clock more than a consistent bedtime does. Pick a time you can keep on weekends too, within about an hour. After a week or two, you will start feeling sleepy at a regular time in the evening.

## 2. Get morning light

Go outside for a few minutes soon after you wake up, even on a cloudy day. Daylight tells your internal clock that the day has started, which helps you feel sleepy at the right time that night.

## 3. Set a caffeine cutoff

Caffeine stays in your system for hours; its half-life is around five hours for many people. A 3 p.m. coffee can still be working at bedtime. Try making your last cup before noon or early afternoon and see whether you fall asleep faster.

## 4. Keep the bedroom cool, dark and quiet

A slightly cool room helps most people sleep; many sleep experts suggest roughly 16 to 19 °C (60 to 67 °F). Blackout curtains or an eye mask handle light. A fan or white noise covers street sounds.

## 5. Build a wind-down routine

Give yourself 30 to 60 minutes before bed to shift gears. Dim the lights, put work away, and do something calm: reading a paper book, stretching, a shower. Phones are the hardest part. Charging yours outside the bedroom removes the temptation to scroll.

## 6. Get up if you cannot sleep

If you have been lying awake for what feels like 20 minutes, get up and do something quiet in dim light until you feel sleepy, then go back to bed. This keeps your brain from linking the bed with frustration.

## When to see a doctor

Talk to a doctor if poor sleep lasts more than a few weeks, if you snore loudly or wake up gasping, or if you feel very tired during the day despite enough time in bed. Insomnia and sleep apnea are common and treatable.

=== health | A Beginner's Strength Routine You Can Do at Home | beginner-strength-routine-home
DEK: Twenty minutes, twice a week, with no equipment. Five moves that cover your whole body and get easier to progress as you get stronger.
QUERY: woman exercising home living room
Health guidelines in many countries recommend muscle-strengthening activity at least two days a week, alongside regular aerobic exercise. You do not need a gym to start. This routine uses your body weight and a sturdy chair.

## The routine

Do each exercise for the reps listed, rest about a minute, then move to the next. Go through the full circuit two or three times.

1. **Chair squat, 10 reps.** Stand in front of a chair, feet hip-width apart. Sit back until you lightly touch the seat, then stand up. Keep your chest up and knees in line with your toes.
2. **Wall or counter push-up, 8 to 12 reps.** Hands on a wall or kitchen counter, body in a straight line. Lower your chest toward it, then push away. The lower the surface, the harder it gets.
3. **Glute bridge, 12 reps.** Lie on your back with knees bent. Squeeze your glutes and lift your hips until your body forms a line from shoulders to knees. Lower slowly.
4. **Split squat, 8 reps per leg.** One foot forward, one back. Lower your back knee toward the floor and come back up. Hold a chair for balance if needed.
5. **Plank, 20 to 30 seconds.** Forearms on the floor, body straight, belly braced. Drop to your knees to make it easier.

## How to get stronger

Once a set feels easy, make it harder. Add two reps, slow the lowering phase to three seconds, or move to a harder version: a push-up on a lower surface, a squat without the chair. Small, steady increases are how strength builds.

## Form over speed

Move slowly and with control. Mild muscle soreness a day or two later is normal when you start. Sharp pain is not; stop the exercise that causes it.

## Fit it into your week

Pick two fixed days, such as Monday and Thursday, and put them in your calendar like appointments. Twenty minutes is short enough to do before work or while dinner cooks.

If you have a heart condition, joint problems, or have not exercised in a long time, check with a doctor or physiotherapist before you begin.

=== tech | Set Up a Password Manager in One Afternoon | password-manager-setup
DEK: Reused passwords are how most accounts get taken over. A password manager fixes that, and the setup is easier than you think.
QUERY: laptop keyboard desk
When one website leaks its passwords, attackers try those same email and password pairs on banks, email and shopping sites. If you reuse passwords, one breach can unlock many accounts. A password manager lets you use a different strong password everywhere while remembering only one.

## Choose a manager

The browser and phone you already use may have one built in: Apple Passwords, Google Password Manager, and the Microsoft Edge manager are free and sync across your devices. Dedicated apps such as Bitwarden, 1Password and Proton Pass work across every platform and browser, which helps if you mix Apple, Windows and Android. Bitwarden has a free tier; 1Password is paid.

## Make one great master password

Your master password is the one you have to remember, so make it long rather than complicated. A passphrase of four or five random words, like "orbit pencil marble lantern cove", is both strong and memorable. Never use it anywhere else.

## Turn on two-factor authentication

Protect the password manager itself with two-factor authentication, using an authenticator app or a hardware key. Save the recovery codes it gives you on paper, somewhere safe at home.

## Import what you already have

Most managers can import passwords saved in your browser. Then install the browser extension and phone app so it can fill logins for you.

## Fix the important accounts first

Do not try to change everything at once. Start with the accounts that matter most:

1. Your main email account, because it can reset every other password
2. Banking and payment accounts
3. Your phone's account (Apple ID or Google account)
4. Social media and shopping sites with saved cards

For each one, let the manager generate a new random password and save it. Many managers also flag reused or breached passwords so you can work through the rest over the next few weeks.

## Plan for emergencies

Some managers let you name an emergency contact who can request access if something happens to you. It is worth setting up for the person who handles your affairs.

=== tech | A 15-Minute Privacy Checkup for Your Phone | phone-privacy-checkup
DEK: Apps collect more than they need. Fifteen minutes in your settings can cut back location tracking, ad profiling and apps you forgot you installed.
QUERY: smartphone in hand
Your phone knows where you go, who you talk to and what you look at. Most of that data sharing is controlled by settings you can change. Set a timer and work through this list.

## Location (5 minutes)

Open your location settings and review the list of apps.

- On iPhone: Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Location Services.
- On Android: Settings, then Location, then App location permissions.

Set most apps to "While Using" or "Never". Very few apps need location all the time. Turn off precise location for apps like weather that only need your city.

## App permissions (4 minutes)

Check which apps can use your camera, microphone, contacts and photos. If a flashlight app wants your contacts, remove the permission, or the app. Both iPhone and Android let you give apps access to selected photos instead of your whole library.

## Ad tracking (2 minutes)

- On iPhone: go to Privacy & Security, then Tracking, and turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track".
- On Android: go to Settings, then Privacy, then Ads, and delete your advertising ID.

This limits how easily companies can link your activity across apps.

## Delete apps you do not use (2 minutes)

Unused apps can still run in the background, hold permissions and receive updates. Scroll through your home screens and delete anything you have not opened in months.

## Lock screen and updates (2 minutes)

Use a six-digit passcode or longer, and turn on automatic software updates. Updates fix security holes, and a strong lock screen protects everything if your phone is lost. Check that notifications on the lock screen do not show message previews if other people often see your phone.

Repeat the checkup every few months, or whenever you install a batch of new apps.

=== tech | Back Up Your Photos the Right Way | back-up-photos-3-2-1
DEK: Your photos are probably the most irreplaceable thing you own. The 3-2-1 rule is a simple, proven way to make sure a lost phone or dead drive does not take them with it.
QUERY: external hard drive photos computer
Phones get lost, laptops get stolen, and hard drives fail, often without warning. If your photos live in one place, they are one accident away from gone. The 3-2-1 backup rule is the standard answer.

## The 3-2-1 rule

- **3 copies** of your photos: the original plus two backups.
- **2 different types of storage**, such as your computer and an external drive.
- **1 copy offsite**, somewhere other than your home, usually the cloud.

A fire or burglary can take your laptop and the backup drive sitting next to it. The offsite copy covers that.

## Step 1: Turn on cloud backup

iCloud Photos, Google Photos, Amazon Photos for Prime members, and OneDrive all back up your phone automatically. Free storage tiers are small, so a paid plan with enough space for your library is usually needed. This is your offsite copy.

Check one setting carefully. Some services sync rather than back up, which means deleting a photo on your phone deletes it in the cloud too. Learn how your service handles deletions and how long it keeps deleted items.

## Step 2: Make a local copy

Buy an external drive with at least twice the space your library uses today. Copy your full library to it, either by hand every month or with automatic tools such as Time Machine on a Mac or File History on Windows.

## Step 3: Test a restore

A backup you have never opened is a hope, not a backup. Once a year, pick a few photos from different years and open them from the external drive and from the cloud. If they open, the system works.

## Organize as you go

Delete blurry shots and duplicates every few months, and favorite the photos you love. A smaller, cleaner library is cheaper to store and easier to back up.

=== advice | How to Say No Without Burning Bridges | how-to-say-no
DEK: Saying yes to everything means saying no to your own priorities by default. A few honest phrases make declining feel normal, for you and for the person asking.
QUERY: two people talking coffee table
Every yes spends time you could have used for something else. Learning to say no kindly protects your week, your energy and, often, the relationship itself, because people trust a yes more from someone who sometimes says no.

## Buy yourself time

You do not have to answer on the spot. "Let me check my calendar and get back to you tomorrow" gives you room to decide based on your priorities instead of the pressure of the moment.

## Keep it short and clear

Long explanations invite negotiation. A simple, warm sentence is enough:

- "Thanks for thinking of me, but I can't take this on right now."
- "That sounds great, but my plate is full this month."
- "I'm going to pass this time."

You do not owe a detailed reason. "It doesn't work for me" is a complete answer.

## Offer an alternative when you mean it

If you want to help in a smaller way, say so: "I can't run the event, but I can share it with my network," or "I can't do Saturday, but I could do coffee next week." Only offer what you are happy to deliver.

## Saying no at work

When your manager asks you to take on more, make the trade-off visible: "I can do this. Which of my current projects should move back to make room?" It keeps you helpful while making the cost of the request clear.

## Expect a little discomfort

The first few times will feel awkward, and some people may be disappointed. That is fine. Most people take a polite no in stride and forget about it far sooner than you will.

## Practice on small things

Start with low-stakes requests, like an extra newsletter or a sales call. Each small no makes the important ones easier.

=== advice | Build an Emergency Fund on an Ordinary Income | build-emergency-fund
DEK: A cushion of savings turns a car repair or a lost job from a crisis into an inconvenience. Here is how to build one, starting with a small first goal.
QUERY: coins jar savings
An emergency fund is money set aside only for unexpected costs: a broken boiler, a medical bill, a gap between jobs. Without one, surprises often end up on a credit card, where interest makes them more expensive.

## How much do you need?

A common target is three to six months of essential expenses: rent or mortgage, utilities, food, insurance, transport and minimum debt payments. Add those up for one month and multiply.

That number can feel huge, so start smaller. A first goal of $500 or $1,000 covers many common emergencies and builds the habit.

## Where to keep it

Keep the fund separate from your everyday account so you are not tempted to spend it, but easy to reach when you need it. A high-yield savings account at a bank insured by your country's deposit protection scheme is a good fit: it earns some interest and you can usually access it within a day or two.

## Make saving automatic

Set up an automatic transfer on payday, even a small one. Saving $25 a week adds up to $1,300 in a year without you having to think about it. Increase the amount whenever you get a raise or pay off a debt.

## Find the money

- **Windfalls:** put part of any tax refund, bonus or gift straight into the fund.
- **Subscriptions:** cancel one or two you rarely use and redirect the money.
- **Round-ups:** some banks round each card purchase up to the next dollar and move the change to savings.

## Use it, then refill it

When a real emergency happens, use the fund. That is what it is for. Then restart your automatic transfers to build it back up.

Keep a separate savings pot for predictable costs like holidays, car maintenance and annual subscriptions, so the emergency fund stays for true surprises.

=== advice | How to Make Friends as an Adult | make-friends-as-an-adult
DEK: After school and university, friendship stops happening by default. It takes repeated time together, a bit of courage and one simple habit.
QUERY: friends laughing dinner table
Friendships used to form on their own, because school and university put us in the same rooms as the same people week after week. As adults, work, family and moves break up that rhythm. Friendship still forms the same way; you just have to create the conditions.

## Show up to the same thing, repeatedly

Familiarity builds through repeated, casual contact. A weekly class, a running club, a volunteer shift, a community garden or a regular pub quiz puts you around the same faces over and over. One-off events rarely lead anywhere; the fourth week of the same pottery class often does.

Choose something you would enjoy even if you made no friends there. That keeps you coming back.

## Be the one who invites

Most people are waiting for someone else to make the first move. Be that person. After a few conversations, try: "A few of us are getting a coffee after this, want to come?" or "I'm going to that market on Saturday, do you want to join?"

A specific plan with a time and place is easier to say yes to than "we should hang out sometime."

## Follow up

After a good conversation, send a message within a day or two: a link to the restaurant you mentioned, a photo of the dog you talked about. Small follow-ups show interest without pressure.

## Reconnect with people you already know

Old friends, former colleagues and neighbors are often the easiest place to start. A short "I was thinking of you, how have you been?" is welcome far more often than people expect.

## Be patient

Moving from acquaintance to close friend takes many hours together over months. Some invitations will not lead anywhere, and that is normal. Keep showing up, keep inviting, and the friendships that stick will be worth it.
