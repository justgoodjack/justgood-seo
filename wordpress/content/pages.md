=== about | About Me
Hi, I'm Justin Goode, and Just Good is my magazine for living a little better.

I started this site because most of the advice I needed day to day was scattered across forums, sales pages and articles that took ten paragraphs to get to the point. I wanted one place that answers practical questions clearly: how to pack lighter, sleep better, keep my accounts safe, and handle the awkward parts of work, money and friendship.

## What Just Good covers

Everything here fits into four columns:

- **Travel:** smarter trips, lighter bags and places worth the journey.
- **Health:** small, steady habits for sleeping, moving and feeling better.
- **Tech:** plain-English help with the devices and apps you use every day.
- **Advice:** money, work and people, with practical next steps.

Our tagline sums it up: smart travel, better health, useful tech, and real advice.

## What you can expect

**Useful over clever.** Every story should leave you with something you can do today, whether that is a packing list, a settings checklist or a sentence you can use to turn down a request.

**Plain language.** No jargon for its own sake. If a term needs explaining, I explain it.

**Honesty about limits.** I am not a doctor, financial adviser or lawyer, and I will say so when a topic needs a professional. Health and money stories point to the evidence I relied on and suggest when to get expert help.

**Corrections when I get something wrong.** If you spot an error, tell me and I will fix it.

**Clear disclosures.** If I ever earn money from a link, a review or a partnership, I will say so on the page. You can read the details in the [Disclaimer](/disclaimer/).

## Say hello

I read every message. Questions, story ideas and corrections are all welcome on the [Contact](/contact/) page.

Thanks for reading,
Justin

=== privacy-policy | Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what information justgoodworld.com ("Just Good", "I", "me") collects when you visit, how it is used, and the choices you have. I have tried to keep it short and accurate. If anything here changes, I will update this page and the date above.

## The short version

- Just Good does not run advertising and does not use Google Analytics or any other visitor-tracking analytics.
- The site collects information only when you leave a comment, send an email, or subscribe to the newsletter, plus the basic technical logs every web server keeps.
- I do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

## Information collected automatically

**Server logs.** Just Good is hosted by Hostinger. Like almost every web host, Hostinger's servers record basic technical details for each request, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. These logs are used for security and to keep the site running, and they are kept according to Hostinger's own retention practices.

**Hostinger Reach script.** The site loads a small script from Hostinger's servers (cdn-reach.hostinger.com) that powers the newsletter sign-up. Loading it means your browser connects to Hostinger and shares standard request details such as your IP address. See [Hostinger's privacy policy](https://www.hostinger.com/legal/privacy-policy) for how they handle this.

**Search data.** The site is connected to Google Search Console through the Rank Math SEO plugin. This gives me summary information about how the site appears in Google search results, such as which searches lead to it. It does not identify individual visitors.

## Information you choose to give

**Comments.** When you leave a comment, the site stores the name, email address and website you enter, plus your IP address and browser details to help detect spam. Your name, website and comment are public; your email address is never published. An anonymized string created from your email address (a "hash") may be sent to the Gravatar service to see whether you have a profile picture. Gravatar is run by Automattic; their [privacy policy](https://automattic.com/privacy/) applies. Comments and their details are kept indefinitely so that conversations stay readable, unless you ask me to remove them.

**Email.** If you email me, I receive your email address and anything you include in your message. I use it only to reply and keep it only as long as needed for that conversation.

**Newsletter.** If you subscribe, your email address (and your name, if the form asks for it) is stored with Hostinger Reach, the email service that sends the newsletter. It is used only to send you Just Good emails. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing stops all further emails.

## Cookies

Cookies are small text files saved by your browser. Just Good uses very few:

- **Comment cookies.** If you tick the box when commenting, your name, email and website are saved in your browser for one year so you do not have to type them again.
- **Login cookies.** These are set only for people who log in to manage the site.
- **Third-party services.** The Hostinger Reach script may set cookies needed for the sign-up form to work.

There are no advertising or tracking cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

## Embedded content

Some articles may include embedded content from other websites, such as videos or maps. Embedded content behaves exactly as if you had visited the other website, which may collect data about you under its own privacy policy.

## Who your information is shared with

Your information is shared only with the services that make the site work: Hostinger (hosting and newsletter), Automattic (Gravatar) and Google (search data, which does not include your personal details). It may also be disclosed if the law requires it. It is never sold.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the personal information held about you, correct it, ask for it to be deleted, or object to how it is used. Residents of the European Union, United Kingdom and some US states, including California, have specific rights under laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA. Wherever you live, you can ask me to:

Email [hello@justgoodworld.com](mailto:hello@justgoodworld.com) and I will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

## Children

Just Good is written for adults and is not directed at children under 16. I do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has shared personal information here, please contact me and I will delete it.
